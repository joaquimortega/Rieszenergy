## Verdict

The earlier model gap is closed for the manuscript's Sobolev WCE claim. The checked unit ball now ranges over **all** `Lp ℝ 2 sigma` equivalence classes whose actual harmonic Fourier coefficients have the manuscript's summable weighted square norm at most one. For every such class and `s>1`, Lean constructs a unique continuous representative, proves equality of its Fourier coefficients and every weighted norm term, and defines quadrature by evaluating that representative. `l2SobolevWCE_eq` proves equality of the set of attainable absolute errors, and hence equality of the genuine `L²` WCE with the earlier continuous spectral WCE. The upper Diamond rate, the universal matching lower rate, and the energy comparison transfer without a new analytic hypothesis.

The harmonic eigenspace convention is also tied to a differential operator independent of the chosen basis. `HarmonicLaplacian` descends three actual rotation generators to polynomial functions on the sphere: an ambient polynomial vanishing on the sphere has zero tangential rotation derivative there. Their three squared generators define `spherePolynomialLaplacian` on that polynomial core. `HarmonicCasimir` computes its eigenvalue as `-ℓ(ℓ+1)` on degree-`ℓ` harmonic restrictions. `HarmonicL2Laplacian` proves integration-by-parts symmetry for normalized surface measure and defines the corresponding weak graph by testing against **every** polynomial sphere function. Its `weakSphereLaplacian_eigen_iff` proves that the weak `L²` eigenspace at `-ℓ(ℓ+1)` consists exactly of the `L²` classes of degree-`ℓ` homogeneous harmonic polynomial restrictions. The converse uses genuine `L²` Fourier completeness to exclude extra weak eigenfunctions.

`HarmonicLaplacianEigenspace.spherePolynomialLaplacian_eigen_iff_unconditional` adds the exact strong eigenspace characterization **on the polynomial core itself**, without a chosen basis in its statement. Its forward direction passes a polynomial eigenvector to the weak `L²` graph, invokes the reviewed weak eigenspace theorem, then uses injectivity of the continuous-to-`L²` map to recover equality of the actual sphere functions. The reverse direction is the Casimir identity for homogeneous harmonic restrictions. It neither assumes the desired strong converse nor extends the operator beyond its polynomial core.

This is an intrinsic angular-field sphere Laplacian on a polynomial test core, with exact weak `L²` eigenspaces. The formalization does not identify it with a separate general-manifold Laplace–Beltrami API or construct a maximal self-adjoint operator domain. Neither is required for the spectral Sobolev norm and WCE stated in the corollary. The source also does not package the Sobolev classes as a standalone Hilbert-space type with a completeness theorem; it formalizes their defining spectral unit ball and the exact WCE over it. These qualifications should be retained in any claim of scope.

## Comparison, rate, and lower bound

The existing distance-kernel proof identifies the scalar shifted-Legendre coefficients, exchanges the moment series with justified summable control, and obtains the exact ordered-pair normalized deficit `continuousEnergy (2s−2) − energy X (2s−2)/N²`. With `α=2s−2`, the main theorem's deficit `O(N^(1−α/2))`, divided by `N²` and square-rooted, gives `O(N^(−s/2))` for `1<s<2`. `l2_sobolev_corollary` transfers this to every `L²` spectral class in the unit ball, uniformly over every phase assignment and every `N≥4`.
