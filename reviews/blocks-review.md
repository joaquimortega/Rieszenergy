# Independent review: latitude block scaffold

**Reviewer model:** `gpt-6-sol`.

**Scope:** `BEMOCFormalization/{BandErrors,Taylor,KernelDerivatives,ComparableBlocks,UnequalBlocks,Latitude}.lean`, compared with `definitive.tex` § “Bound of A_{α,N}” and Appendix 2, labels `eq:latitude-decomposition`, `lem:blocks`, `eq:comparableblock`, `eq:farblock`, `eq:oppositeblock`, and `lem:latitude`. I also checked dependent definitions in `Construction`, `Geometry`, `Core`, and `EnergyDecomposition`. This is a review of the *statements and proof architecture*. The `Prop` contracts do not yet constitute analytic proofs.

## Overall assessment

The six modules faithfully state the paper's latitude decomposition and block estimates, including ordered pairs, zero-based to one-based index conversion, the equal/neighbor cusp, polar endpoints, the central band, and the factor `M^(-8)` in the opposite-scale bound. The target `LatitudeBound` uses absolute value, which is stronger than the paper's upper bound for `A` and is exactly what the absolute block summation proves. Constants are existential and independent of `N` and phases. The `N≥1024` restriction in block bounds agrees with the *global* proof convention stated in `definitive.tex` around line 274; my earlier audit's suggestion that the manuscript omitted it locally is only a recommendation for local clarity, not a mathematical defect.

## Blockers before claiming a proof

1. **Local-to-rectangle extension.** `SeparatedDerivativeBound` supplies, for each separated `(s,t)`, an existential `G,W` with a local `C⁴` extension and a pointwise mixed derivative bound. `MixedTaylorBound` expects a *single* `C⁴` function on an open neighborhood of the full closed rectangle, with a uniform derivative bound. The proof must glue the local extensions by their agreement with `latitudeKernel` on the physical square, show derivatives agree on overlaps (including relative boundary points by continuity), and obtain a suitable open neighborhood of the compact rectangle. Alternatively strengthen the analytic contract to state a rectangle-level extension directly. Pointwise existence alone cannot be passed into `MixedTaylorBound` by direct application.
2. **Polar derivative totalization.** `polarKernel` is a real power of an expression defined for all real `θ,φ,ψ`; the bound only assumes its base is positive at the evaluation point. That is sufficient locally, but proofs using `iteratedDeriv` must establish an open positive-base neighborhood and identify the derivative of the Euclidean distance power there. At `D=0`, the derivative formula is unavailable, as intended. For `latitudeKernel`, square roots at `s=±1` are totalized by Lean but are not differentiable in the ambient real variable. The separated-series extension is therefore necessary at poles; differentiation of the raw square-root expression at a pole would be invalid.
3. **Adjacent and diagonal blocks.** The full averaged kernel generally is not `C⁴` across `s=t` for `0<α<2`. The comparable proof must introduce the truncated angular kernels at `θ=1/r`; apply total variation to the low-angle part and `MixedTaylorBound` to the high-angle part. The `α=1` logarithm in the `α-2` integral is a separate branch. No generic derivative bound on the untruncated diagonal should be assumed.
4. **Source bridge and finite partitions.** `LatitudeIdentity` has no proof yet: show the normalized sphere-height disintegration, both band mass and first-moment formulas, kernel symmetry, the constant-potential identity, and finite expansion with cancellation. To derive `LatitudeBound`, prove ordered-pair trichotomy with a swap for `r_j>8r_k`, then finite row and cubic scale sums. The central band is excluded from `SameSide` and must enter `OppositeBlockBound` when noncomparable.

## Statement-level observations

- `BandMoments`, `LatitudeIdentity`, and `BlockSymmetry` are intentionally unrestricted in `α` except that the latter two take it as a parameter. Their paper proofs require `0<α<2` (at least `α>0` for regular integrability at zero). This is not a logical contradiction because one can prove/use them under an external hypothesis, but explicit hypotheses on the contracts would make the API honest and prevent a failed attempt to prove every real exponent. `BlockSymmetry` likely holds more broadly wherever the integrals exist; the actual Lean integral totalization should be checked.
- `MixedTaylorBound` is stated for every `N≥4` and a global `ContDiffOn ℝ 4 G W`, which is sufficient and somewhat stronger than the manuscript's `G∈C⁴(B_j×B_k)`. Its constant `r_j³r_k³/M⁸` follows from exact band width `2r_j/N`, TV at most `2r_j`, and `N≥4M²`. Make sure the derivative operator's order matches the product Taylor calculation; mixed partials commute under `C⁴`.
- The `Comparable` predicate uses real casts and two inequalities exactly matching `r_j/8≤r_k≤8r_j`. The `(1+|(j.val:ℝ)-k.val|)^(α-3)` distance is unchanged by converting both labels from zero-based to one-based. `SameSide` correctly uses one-based indices and strict exclusion of the central index `M`.
- `SmallSizeBound` quantifies over all `Phases N`, hence its constant is uniform over continuously many phase choices. It cannot be justified solely by saying there are finitely many `N`. A direct uniform bound `diamondEnergy≤4^α N²` (or `2^α N²` per pair), `continuousEnergy α N²` fixed, and `scale α N>0` on the finite range suffices. Prove the nonnegative deficit separately through `DiamondNonnegative`.
- `latitudeKernel` uses a `[0,2π]` integral, whereas Appendix 2 switches to `[0,π]` by cosine symmetry. An explicit equality is needed before importing angular estimates. Likewise, `polarKernel` uses an `α/2` power of squared distance; the source uses `D^α`. Equality holds on nonnegative squared distance and must be shown before transferring derivatives.
- `LatitudeBound` follows only after `0<α<2`, since `∑(1+ℓ)^(α-3)` converges precisely because `3-α>1`. The current contract is parameterized without carrying the range, which is acceptable for an unproved predicate but the deriving theorem should state the range explicitly.

## Reuse and recommendations

The legacy tree contains useful proof patterns, but its construction and index types differ and should not be imported blindly. Inspect `legacy/BEMOCFormalization/LatitudeBands.lean` (`bandError_one`, `bandError_id`, `bandError_affine`) for two-moment algebra; `MixedTaylorRemainder.lean` (`abs_bandPairError_le_of_mixedDerivative`) for the double Taylor inequality; `LatitudeDecomposition.lean` for finite energy expansion; `LatitudePairClassification.lean` for regime partition; `LatitudePolarBlocks.lean` for endpoint TV handling; and `LatitudeUnequalSeries.lean` for geometric-series estimates. Each candidate needs a type and normalization translation, particularly the new `r_M` and `N` formula and the new `RingIndex`.

Recommended proof order: first `BandMoments`, symmetry, and exact latitude identity; next a reusable double Taylor lemma; then separated series and polar angular estimates; then the comparable cases; then same-side and opposite/central unequal cases; finally finite summation. Keep every unproved analytic fact as an accurately quantified `Prop` target until its proof lands. The final theorem must also import the longitude estimate and nonnegative energy fact; neither follows from this latitude audit.
