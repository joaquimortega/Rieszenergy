## Latitude coverage and assembly

`Comparable` is symmetric real eightfold population comparability. Its negation yields exactly one of `8r_j<r_k` and `8r_k<r_j`; `LatitudeSummation.block_le_three_majorants` handles both orientations, using `BlockSymmetry` to reverse the latter. All four component bounds and `near_tail_derivative_bound` are theorem applications with hypotheses only `0<α<2`; `comparable_block_bound` has no residual derivative assumption.

`LatitudeIdentity.latitudeIdentity_of_pos` expresses `latitudeError` as the negative ordered double sum of the actual `kernelBlock`, using the concrete height potential and band quadrature. `LatitudeSummation.latitude_bound_of_blocks` bounds its absolute value by the sum of block absolute values. The arithmetic conversion yields `N^(1−α/2)` with a constant independent of `N` and phases. The latitude term itself is phase-independent. The longitude bound retains `∀ φ`, so the final upper bound remains phase-uniform. For `4≤N<1024`, `small_size_bound` gives a phase-uniform bound directly from nonnegative point energy and `scale α N≥1`; it does not extrapolate a large-`N` estimate.

`Corollaries.cap_corollary` applies `main_theorem` at `α=1` together with the proved geometric Stolarsky identity and universal lower bound. Its proposition quantifies the same two positive constants over every `N≥4` and every phase assignment. Thus the cap result has no residual assumption on the energy theorem or on an external Beck estimate.
