## Kernel and derivative chain

- The physical angular data are `A=2-2st` and `B=2√(1-s²)√(1-t²)`. `SeparatedKernel` proves the physical ratio bound `B≤(1-ε)A` implies `separatedRatio = B²/A² ≤ (1-ε)²` when `A>0`. `SeparatedAngularExpansion` identifies the actual angular integral with the even binomial series under `|B/A|<1`, including its normalization by `2π`. Odd cosine moments vanish by a shift through π; the uniform absolute moment bound justifies the even/odd `tsum` split.
- Each `separatedEvenPowerSummand` uses natural powers of `1-s²` and `1-t²`, so its finite derivative formulas are meaningful even when a radius vanishes. `SeparatedSummandDerivatives` proves that its explicit `DSSTT` is the actual `(2,2)` iterated derivative wherever `A>0`. `SeparatedSeriesBounds` treats modes `m≥2` with a geometric `q^(m-2)` majorant, handles modes `0` and `1` separately, and provides one rectangle-wide summable majorant for the four successive derivative stages. `SeparatedSeriesDifferentiation` uses these bounds for two left derivatives followed by two right derivatives of the series.
- `separatedSmoothExtension` is a single function of `(s,t)` on the plane, restricted to the open algebraic domain `separatedOpen` where `A>0` and the absolute series ratio is below one. It is `C⁴` there and equals `latitudeKernel` at **all physical points** in the domain, including polar endpoints. The extension is not obtained by gluing point-dependent witnesses; thus the earlier local-to-rectangle concern in `blocks-review.md` is resolved for this branch.
- `SeparatedMixedFourth` identifies `mixedFourth` of that extension with the normally convergent explicit fourth-derivative series on the interior of any `SeparatedSeriesRectangle`. The proof swaps coordinates because `mixedFourth` differentiates the right variable first while the series derivative chain takes the left variable first. Symmetry of `A`, the ratio, the extension, and the rectangle data supports the swap. The resulting numerical bound has the precise scale `A^(α/2-4)` and constant `separatedSeriesFourthConstant α q`.
- `SeparatedLocalRectangle` produces a closed physical rectangle around any **interior** point with strict `separatedRatio<q<1`, retaining a positive lower bound for `A` and the same `q` over the whole rectangle. `SeparatedComplete` chooses `q₀=(1-ε)²<q₁<q₂<1`: `q₂` fixes one constant independent of the point, `q₁` permits local rectangles at nearby interior points, and the `q₀<q₁` slack permits polar endpoint transfer by continuity of the fourth derivative. The final `SeparatedDerivativeBound` witness always uses the same `separatedSmoothExtension α` and `separatedOpen`, even though its contract allows point-dependent witnesses. It states and proves exactly the contract's `A>0` and angular-gap premises.

## Unequal block contracts

`SameSide` means both one-based band indices are strictly north of the central index or both strictly south. Its negation includes the central band. Both block contracts orient the population inequality as `8 r_j<r_k`, quantify over `N≥1024`, and bound the absolute `kernelBlock`; they impose no order on the indices beyond their populations. `small_unequal_polar_half` shows the smaller population cannot be central and lies in the polar half of one hemisphere. The northern cases are proved directly; simultaneous north–south reflection supplies their southern counterparts without changing `st`, the squared radii, population, or the kernel's angular gap.

For same-side unequal rectangles, `sameSide_unequal_rectangle_separated` proves `A>0` and the fixed angular gap `B≤(255/256)A` throughout the **closed** band product. `sameSide_unequal_radial_lower` also supplies `A≥(r_k/M)²/64`. For opposite or central rectangles, `opposite_unequal_rectangle_separated` proves the same angular gap and the absolute lower bound `A≥8/5`. Both regimes therefore lie inside `separatedOpen`, including endpoints, and the one extension agrees with the physical latitude kernel on their entire band products.

`uniformSeparatedFourthBound` specializes the completed separated theorem to `ε=1/256`, with a constant independent of `N`, indices, heights, and phase choices. `kernelBlock_le_of_global_extension` uses the already-proved `mixedTaylorBound` only after it has a single `C⁴` extension on an open neighborhood of the whole closed rectangle and equality with `latitudeKernel` there. Since `α/2-4<0`, the two lower bounds for `A` turn the derivative scale into respectively `(r_k/M)^(α-8)` and `(8/5)^(α/2-4)`. `far_block_scale_identity` gives the exact same-side factor `64^(4-α/2) r_j³/(M^α r_k^(5-α))`; the opposite factor is `r_j³r_k³/M⁸`. The existential constants in `unequalBlockBounds` are positive and absorb only these fixed factors. Thus the proved conclusions match the definitions in `UnequalBlocks.lean`, including the central case under `¬SameSide`.
