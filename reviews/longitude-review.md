## Coverage

`FourierDecay.lean` names the exact real cusp profile, its normalized cosine coefficient, monotonicity in the smoothing parameter, the Gamma expression, and uniform `n^(-1-2a)` decay. The Gamma expression agrees with Appendix 1, including its negative sign and factor `2^(-a)`. Since the profile is even, this cosine coefficient agrees with the real Fourier coefficient at positive `n`, but that identification still needs a theorem before using complex Fourier inversion. `δ=0` appears in `FourierDomination`, `CuspCoefficientFormula`, and `FourierDecayBound`; negative frequencies would follow from evenness. The conditions `0<a<1` are absent from the definitions themselves and must be hypotheses of every proof/implication theorem.

`Trapezoid.lean` retains `B=0`, `A=B`, `L=1`, arbitrary real phase, and a single constant outside all quantified geometric variables. Its `GridMultiplicity` is exactly the ordered double-grid identity needed in the manuscript. The right side uses `gcd(q,r)` and `lcm(q,r)` with the correct factor. `Function.Periodic f (2π)` and all positive cardinality assumptions suffice mathematically. The definition does not assert continuity or integrability of arbitrary `f`, which is appropriate because the identity is finite and algebraic. The separate `TrapezoidBound` must be derived from `FourierDecayBound` through a Fourier inversion/root-grid argument; an import alone does not establish that implication.

`Longitude.lean` uses the manuscript's exact gcd weight and requests `O(T²)`, then asks for an absolute, phase-uniform bound on `longitudeError α N φ` at scale `N^(1-α/2)`. `longitudeError` in `EnergyDecomposition.lean` is the difference between the weighted ring energy and the ordered-pair point energy. This preserves diagonal pairs; for `α>0` they vanish in the point energy. `GcdSumBound` can be proved for `α>0`; `LongitudeBound` needs `0<α<2` to match the paper and the geometric-to-scale step. The definition is unconditional as a proposition: it does not say that it follows from `TrapezoidBound`, `GridMultiplicity`, `GeometryBounds`, `ConstructionFacts`, and `GcdSumBound`. Add a separate conditional theorem with these explicit assumptions, then ultimately discharge them if a fully verified result is intended.

## Blocking proof obligations and cautions

* The analytic chain is open: binomial positivity or another smoothing argument, endpoint `δ=0` handling, Gamma/beta coefficient evaluation (or a replacement cusp estimate), absolute Fourier summability, Fourier inversion, and phase-shifted aliasing. `FourierDomination` and `CuspCoefficientFormula` do not by themselves imply `FourierDecayBound` without a Gamma-ratio estimate uniform in `n`.
* The finite pair estimate must carry the `gcd(q,r)` multiplicity through `q*r=d*L`; its final gcd exponent is `1+α`, not `α`. This is not yet a theorem in the new scaffold. The angular kernel for heights must be proved equal to `angularKernel α (2-2st) (2ρ_sρ_t)` and point squared distances must match the phase differences.
* `GeometryBounds` contains the lower comparison `c*M*ρ_j≤r_j`, which is the direction needed for `ρ_j≤r_j/(cM)`. Its population cap and multiplicity field likewise match the required double-sum reduction. `ConstructionFacts` must establish positive populations, valid heights, and the actual point count so that the fallback branch in `point` cannot affect `diamondEnergy`.
* The gcd series step is open. A coprime-factor decomposition is possible, but a divisor majorization may reuse older code. Either approach must prove one constant independent of `T` and handle `T=0` (the current `GcdSumBound` quantifies it; the sum is then empty).
* The imported module graph only provides definitions and a few algebraic lemmas. Files in `Rieszenergy/legacy/BEMOCFormalization/` are useful proof examples, not current source imports. In particular, the legacy construction uses different population formulas in places, so copy its methods only after checking the new definitions.

Recommendation: keep the contracts as honest conditional targets and add implication theorems in the order `FourierDecayBound → TrapezoidBound`, `GridMultiplicity + TrapezoidBound + geometry → pair estimate`, and `pair estimate + GcdSumBound + multiplicity + N/M scale → LongitudeBound`. State `0<a<1` or `0<α<2` in each theorem. The current three Lean modules do not prove the manuscript's longitude bound, but their statements have no identified mathematical sign, exponent, endpoint, or normalization error.
