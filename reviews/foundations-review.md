## Verdict

The scaffold represents the **new** deterministic Diamond configuration faithfully: `M = floor(sqrt(N/4))`, populations `4j` away from the equator, one midpoint polygon per band, symmetric reflection, `2M−1` occupied parallels, arbitrary independent phases, and an ordered double energy sum. `MainTheoremTarget` correctly states a uniform constant for all `N ≥ 4`, all phases, and `0 < α < 2`. The `N ≥ 1024` cutoff is confined to the analytic latitude estimate, with a small-size contract for the finite remainder. The proved `deficit_eq_latitude_add_longitude` and `main_theorem_of_estimates` are useful, but neither constitutes a proof of the theorem: the substantive contracts remain `def ... : Prop`.

## Blockers before claiming formalization

1. **All mathematical obligations are declarations of propositions.** `ConstructionFacts`, `BoundaryFormulas`, `GeometryBounds`, `AngularGeometry`, `SurfaceIntegration`, `ConstantPotential`, `EnergyNonnegative`, `MeasureNegativeType`, `DiamondNonnegative`, and `MainTheoremTarget` have no inhabitants here. The main assembly requires `LatitudeBound`, `LongitudeBound`, `SmallSizeBound`, and `DiamondNonnegative` as hypotheses. A successful elaboration of these files establishes consistency of signatures and the two short algebraic proofs, not the manuscript's theorem.
2. **The manuscript's named set needs a canonical phase bridge.** The manuscript fixes a vertex at positive first coordinate on each circle (§2, lines 315–316); this is `φ = 0` in the current parametrization. Add a named zero-phase configuration and prove its cardinality `N` from `ConstructionFacts`, then derive its theorem from the phase-uniform one. Without that bridge the interface proves a stronger labelled-family statement but does not explicitly identify the named `\mathcal P_N`.
3. **Continuous energy and nonnegativity require separate analytic results.** The chosen `sigma` must be shown to be a probability measure and to obey the height-angle integration formula. Prove `I_α = 2^(α+1)/(α+2)` and constant potential independently of conditional negative type. The latter yields the sign only after cancellation of the cross term against `Nσ`. `EnergyNonnegative` is a finite-configuration consequence; `DiamondNonnegative` then follows by reindexing, with injectivity needed for a set interpretation.
4. **The latitude identity needs a careful measure bridge.** The `BandErrors` functional correctly binds the first band to `s` and second to `t`, fixing the variable/measure swap in `definitive.tex` equation `eq:latitude-decomposition` (around lines 614–619). Establish the height pushforward, exact partition of the continuous height measure (endpoints have zero mass), finite sums/Fubini, and constant-potential cancellation before using the identity. Closed bands are harmless for Lebesgue restriction but would double-count endpoint atoms in a naive measure partition.
5. **Singular and polar blocks cannot be dispatched by the smooth Taylor estimate alone.** At equal or neighboring bands the fourth mixed derivative of `F_α` is singular at coincident heights and angle zero. The appendix splits small angles and handles polar endpoints separately. The `ComparableBlockBound` contract includes these cases; its proof will need those mechanisms, not merely `BandMoments` plus an unrestricted `C⁴` bound.

## Suggested interface corrections and proof priorities

- Make theorem-status documentation explicit: the public target is currently an unproved `Prop`, while the assembly theorem is conditional. Do not advertise a verified energy bound until the contracts are inhabited.
- Add a named `zeroPhases`, finite point set, cardinality theorem, and zero-phase corollary. Keep the all-phase estimate because the angular trapezoid lemma is uniform in phase.
- Prove `ConstructionFacts` and `BoundaryFormulas` for **every** `N ≥ 4`. In particular `4 ≤ N ≤ 15` gives `M=1`, a single equatorial `N`-gon; no proof may assume `M≥16` at construction level. Establish `4M ≤ r_M ≤ 12M+3`, strict boundary descent, midpoint interiors and distinct heights before point injectivity.
- The `population` and `boundary` definitions are total on natural indices, but only the finite `RingIndex N` range carries the manuscript interpretation. Keep validity hypotheses visible in lemmas; `j-1` is safe only after showing the one-based band index is positive.
- Prove the exact chordal identity and `A≥B≥0` for the angular kernel; `A=B` for occupied midpoint rings precisely when the ring indices agree. The base `Real.rpow` at coincident points is zero for `α>0`, so the diagonal in the ordered sum contributes zero.
- `GeometryBounds` includes the manuscript's `r_j ≍ Mρ_j`, `r_M ≤ 15M`, and multiplicity at most three. Its constants can be absolute. The companion `Σρ_j ≍ M` should be exported explicitly if downstream proofs use it; it follows from the population sum and both comparison inequalities.
- For finite-size closure, use uniform bounds over the compact phase torus or a direct phase-independent bound `0 ≤ E ≤ 2^α N²` for `α>0`; finitely many `N` alone do not dispose of infinitely many phase choices.
- Review any import from `legacy/` lemma by lemma. Its construction used `r_j=4j−1` and Simpson-style boundary/midpoint splitting; only generic sphere, negative-type, angular, and abstract two-moment facts can transfer without altering hypotheses.
