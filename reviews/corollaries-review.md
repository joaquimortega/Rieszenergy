## Verdict

The two observable definitions are substantive. `capDiscrepancySq` integrates the empirical cap proportion error over normalized sphere area and ordinary Lebesgue `dt` on `[-1,1]`; it is not defined through energy. `SobolevUnitBall` uses coefficients of a genuine harmonic basis weighted by the manuscript's `(1+ℓ(ℓ+1))^s`, with a `Summable` guard, and `sobolevWCE` is the supremum of actual integration errors. This avoids the principal tautology risk. The exponents in `Corollaries.lean` are correct. No theorem in the new modules yet proves Stolarsky, Beck, harmonic-basis existence, Sobolev embedding, spectral kernel comparison, or lower Sobolev cubature error. Thus `DefinitiveTargets` remains an unproved destination.

## Material blockers and corrections

1. **Stolarsky has two indices.** `StolarskyIdentity` is a universal `Fin n` statement, while `DiamondStolarsky` is stated for `PointIndex N`. The latter follows only after proving the population sum/cardinality in `ConstructionFacts`, constructing `PointIndex N ≃ Fin N`, and proving invariance of cap integrals and ordered energy under relabeling. The equality has the correct factor: `4 N² capDiscrepancySq = deficit` for ordinary `dt`; probability `dt/2` would give factor 8. The diagonal in `Core.energy` is included, but equals zero for exponent 1; do not claim this for arbitrary real exponents without `0<α`.
2. **Beck's domain must match the conclusion.** `BeckLowerBound` assumes injective `Fin n` maps. `ConstructionFacts` includes injectivity of `point N φ`, but the proof must pass through the finite-index equivalence. The external lower theorem is about actual cap discrepancy and must use the same normalized area and `dt` convention; a differing convention changes its constant but not the exponent. The paper's corollary is for deterministic phases; this target claims uniformity over *all* `Phases N`, a stronger statement. Check that the proposed unconditional main energy theorem and the phase-independent estimates really deliver that uniformity.
3. **Supremum totalization.** `sSup` is formally a real term even when the set is empty or unbounded. Before any order theorem involving `sobolevWCE`, prove the set is nonempty using the zero continuous function and `SobolevUnitBall`, and bounded above using `SobolevEmbedding` plus `IsProbabilityMeasure sigma`, positive cardinality, and `|quadratureError|≤2K`. The current `SobolevAssembly` takes `SobolevEmbedding` as an explicit premise, which is honest, but `SobolevEnergyComparison` as a standalone proposition also uses `sobolevWCE` and should be established only after these guards.
4. **Spectral model is mathematical, not yet a Hilbert-space construction.** The carrier in `SobolevUnitBall` is continuous functions with finite spectral square sum. For `s>1` this represents the paper's `H^s` only after proving the Sobolev embedding/continuous representative theorem and a density/completeness result that identifies the Fourier space with the standard `L²`-based Hilbert space. `HarmonicBasis.complete` asks only that no nonzero *continuous* function be orthogonal to every basis vector. With the homogeneous harmonic condition and orthonormality this is a reasonable density contract, but it is not by itself a proved Parseval theorem. In particular, prove basis-independent spectral norm or state why the final universal quantifier over every `Y` is appropriate.
5. **The spectral comparison is the critical bridge.** For `α=2s-2`, the generalized-distance kernel has positive degree-`ℓ` coefficients `a_ℓ(s)`. The actual norm uses `b_ℓ(s)=(1+ℓ(ℓ+1))^{-s}`. To show `WCE_s²≤A_s` times normalized deficit, establish `b_ℓ(s)≤A_s a_ℓ(s)` for all `ℓ≥1`, together with the reproducing-kernel identity and justified sum/integral interchange. Neither the weight comparison nor equation (42) of BSSW14 is encoded by a proof in the scaffold. BSSW14 equation (29), cited by the manuscript at line 186, is the *general* kernel formula; equations (40) and (42) give the specialized distance identity. A constant `A_s` is necessary because the manuscript fixes a different but equivalent spectral norm.
6. **Optimality is separate.** `SobolevOptimality` is correctly an independent lower contract for actual WCE, but `SobolevAssembly` derives only the upper corollary and `DefinitiveTargets` demands optimality without a proof route. Use BSSW14 Theorem 3/Hesse–Sloan, or Wagner's universal energy-deficit lower bound plus the *reverse* spectral comparison. Beck or Wagner is similarly needed for the cap lower bound. The main upper energy theorem cannot yield either lower conclusion.
7. **All-`N` versus eventual result.** `MainTheorem α` and the two corollary targets quantify over every `N≥4`, whereas the manuscript's proof permits a large threshold `N≥1024` and absorbs small `N` into constants. `SmallSizeBound` must supply a uniform bound over all phases on finite `N`, not simply cite finiteness of the number of sizes. For lower bounds the universal external results cover all positive `N` (subject to injectivity), so the small-size issue is mainly upper bounds.
